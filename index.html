01 / PROFILE

About Kelechi

Kelechi Obijiaku is a lawyer and procurement specialist with over 17 years of experience. He advises public institutions and private organisations on commercial agreements, procurement reform and purchasing strategy. He also speaks at professional conferences and leads training for lawyers, procurement officers and suppliers, bringing the perspectives of buyers and bidders into the same conversation.

At the Lagos State Public Procurement Agency, Kelechi led the teams that reviewed the State’s procurement law, developed its implementing regulations and shaped its gender-responsive procurement policy. He also contributed to technical guidance on emergency purchasing and the disposal of public assets. As Deputy Director and State Counsel in the Directorate of Commercial Law at the Lagos State Ministry of Justice, he advises on concessions and public-private partnerships and drafts and reviews agreements for projects in sectors including water, energy and healthcare.

Across these roles, Kelechi helps organisations decide what to buy, how to open competition to capable suppliers and how to structure agreements that can be managed long after signature. His advice connects policy choices to commercial consequences, from the tender condition that determines who can bid to the contract clause that establishes who carries a risk and what happens if delivery falls short.

“Sound institutions depend not only on good laws, but on rules people can understand, apply and be held accountable for.”
02 / SELECTED CONTRIBUTIONS

Shaping procurement in institutions

Kelechi’s work at the Lagos State Public Procurement Agency spans the rules that govern public purchasing and the decisions officers make when applying them. He led the Agency’s review of the procurement law and the development of its implementing regulations. He also led the Lagos technical team that developed the State’s gender-responsive procurement policy. As a technical team member on other projects, he helped develop guidance on matters ranging from emergency purchases to the disposal of public assets.

Team leadLagos State Public Procurement Law, 2021

Kelechi led the Agency team that reviewed the procurement legislation. He drew on the Agency’s experience of applying the law and coordinated the team’s recommendations.

Team leadLagos State Public Procurement Regulations, 2022

Kelechi led the Agency team that developed and reviewed the implementing regulations. The team’s task was to translate the law into procedures that officials could apply consistently and suppliers could understand.

Team leadGender-Responsive Procurement Policy

Kelechi led the Lagos technical team that developed the policy, working with colleagues and UN Women. The team addressed practical questions about how to identify eligible women-owned businesses and track their participation. The State approved the policy in 2025.

Technical team memberProcurement Manual

As a member of the technical team, Kelechi helped develop the manual that procurement officers use in their day-to-day work. It connects the formal rules with decisions officers face when planning a purchase, evaluating bids or documenting an award.

Technical team memberGuidelines on Disposal of Public Assets

Kelechi contributed to the team’s work on how public bodies dispose of assets they no longer need. That work concerned both the value obtained and the record of how the institution reached its decision.

Technical team memberSustainable Procurement Framework

Kelechi served on the team that worked on the framework. It looks beyond the initial purchase price to questions such as what an asset will cost to operate and maintain, and the wider effects of the purchasing decision.

Technical team memberEmergency Procurement Guidelines

Kelechi helped develop the guidelines as a member of the technical team. The challenge was to give officers practical guidance when time is short, including how to record the reasons for acting urgently and the decisions taken.

Technical team memberVision 2036

As a member of the Vision 2036 team, Kelechi brought a procurement practitioner’s perspective to longer-term planning and helped the team consider what the institution would need to achieve its goals.

State workstream coordinatorWorld Bank-supported SFTAS procurement transparency

Kelechi coordinated the Lagos procurement workstream for contract-award disclosure and supporting guidance, training and evidence under the SFTAS programme, including OCDS-aligned publication requirements.

Legal team lead and contributorProcess maps and procurement workflows

Kelechi led legal-department process mapping and contributed to a statewide procurement process flowchart, clarifying approvals, responsibilities, recordkeeping and handoffs across procurement activities.

Legal and process contributorElectronic procurement governance

Kelechi worked with implementation teams to translate procurement rules into electronic workflows, focusing on legal compliance, approval trails, transparency and reliable records. The platform's institutional rollout was an Agency-wide achievement.
