01 / PROFILE

Working where law becomes public policy.

My professional experience spans commercial law, procurement regulation, institutional governance and the development of rules and guidance that shape public-sector decisions. A Nigerian lawyer called to the Bar in 2007, I serve as Deputy Director and State Counsel in the Lagos State Ministry of Justice, following extensive service with the Lagos State Public Procurement Agency.

My work includes leading institutional review and development efforts concerning procurement legislation, regulations and gender-responsive procurement policy. I have also served as a member of the technical team working on the procurement manual, emergency procurement guidelines, guidelines on disposal of public assets, the sustainable procurement framework and Vision 2036.

My commercial-law practice complements that policy experience. At the Lagos State Ministry of Justice, I have advised on concessions, public-private partnerships and complex commercial arrangements, drafting and reviewing contractual documents connected with major public infrastructure and services across healthcare, transport, energy, water, technology and other sectors. I am particularly interested in how laws, administrative systems and incentives affect transparency, market access, public value and development outcomes.

“Sound institutions depend not only on good laws, but on rules people can understand, apply and be held accountable for.”

That Got Me Thinking

26 November 2026

Who keeps the key?

Imagine handing over a new library to a school. The shelves are up, the books are counted and somebody has signed for the keys. On Monday morning, a pupil wants to borrow a book. Who opens the door?

It’s a small question, but I’d like to hear it asked more often when we plan a public project. The handover date matters to the contractor. The first day a child can walk in and pick a book matters to the child. Sometimes there’s a surprising amount of work between those two dates.

If you could add one question to a project handover meeting, what would yours be?
