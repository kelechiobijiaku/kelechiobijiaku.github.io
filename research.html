RESEARCH

Public procurement, institutions and accountability

Affirmative procurement policies

My MSc research examined the impact and efficacy of affirmative procurement policies on women-owned businesses in Lagos State, focusing on access to public contracting.

Contract management

My professional project examines the development of a standardised contract management framework for the Lagos State Public Procurement Agency, including post-award monitoring and accountability.

Research interests

Digital procurement, administrative discretion, institutional accountability, inclusive markets and the relationship between procurement reform and development.
