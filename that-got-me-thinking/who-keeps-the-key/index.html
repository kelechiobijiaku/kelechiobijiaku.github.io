That Got Me Thinking

Who keeps the key?

Kelechi Obijiaku · 26 November 2026

Imagine handing over a new library to a school. The shelves are up, the books are counted and somebody has signed for the keys. On Monday morning, a pupil wants to borrow a book. Who opens the door?

It’s a small question, but I’d like to hear it asked more often when we plan a public project. The handover date matters to the contractor. The first day a child can walk in and pick a book matters to the child. Sometimes there’s a surprising amount of work between those two dates.

If you could add one question to a project handover meeting, what would yours be?
