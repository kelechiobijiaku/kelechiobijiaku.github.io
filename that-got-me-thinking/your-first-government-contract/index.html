How do you win your first government contract?

Kelechi Obijiaku · 24 November 2026

Something about Bangladesh’s procurement reforms got me thinking. Imagine you run a small business in Nigeria. You can supply the desks a school needs, but the tender asks for three similar government contracts. How do you win your first one?

For a bridge, I’d want to know which bridges the contractor has built. For school desks, I’d want to see the quality of the desks and know the supplier can deliver on time. Would it matter that their last three customers were private schools?

Sometimes a fairer tender starts with a better question about experience.
