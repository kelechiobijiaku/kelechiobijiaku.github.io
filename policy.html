02 / SELECTED CONTRIBUTIONS

Shaping procurement in Lagos State

My work with the Lagos State Public Procurement Agency includes legislative review, policy development and practical guidance for public officers.

TEAM LEAD

Lagos State Public Procurement Law, 2021

I led the Agency team that reviewed the procurement legislation. My role was to bring our experience of applying the law into the review and coordinate the team’s recommendations.

TEAM LEAD

Lagos State Public Procurement Regulations, 2022

I led the Agency team that developed and reviewed the implementing regulations. Our task was to translate the law into procedures that officials could apply consistently and suppliers could understand.

TEAM LEAD

Gender-Responsive Procurement Policy

I led the Lagos technical team that developed the policy, working with colleagues and UN Women. We addressed practical questions about how to identify eligible women-owned businesses and track their participation. The State approved the policy in 2025.

TECHNICAL TEAM MEMBER

Procurement Manual

As a member of the technical team, I helped develop the manual that procurement officers use in their day-to-day work. It connects the formal rules with decisions officers face when planning a purchase, evaluating bids or documenting an award.

TECHNICAL TEAM MEMBER

Guidelines on Disposal of Public Assets

I contributed to the team’s work on how public bodies dispose of assets they no longer need. That work concerned both the value obtained and the record of how the institution reached its decision.

TECHNICAL TEAM MEMBER

Sustainable Procurement Framework

I served on the team that worked on the framework. It looks beyond the initial purchase price to questions such as what an asset will cost to operate and maintain, and the wider effects of the purchasing decision.

TECHNICAL TEAM MEMBER

Emergency Procurement Guidelines

I helped develop the guidelines as a member of the technical team. The challenge was to give officers practical guidance when time is short, including how to record the reasons for acting urgently and the decisions taken.

TECHNICAL TEAM MEMBER

Vision 2036

I contributed as a member of the team working on Vision 2036. It was an opportunity to bring a procurement practitioner’s perspective to longer-term planning and consider what the institution would need to deliver its ambitions.

TRANSPARENCY & ACCOUNTABILITY

World Bank-supported SFTAS procurement transparency

Coordinated the Lagos procurement workstream for contract-award disclosure and supporting guidance, training and evidence under the SFTAS programme, including OCDS-aligned publication requirements.

Role: State procurement workstream coordination
INSTITUTIONAL DESIGN

Process maps and procurement workflows

Led legal-department process mapping and contributed to a statewide procurement process flowchart, clarifying approvals, responsibilities, recordkeeping and handoffs across procurement activities.

Role: Process design and practitioner guidance
DIGITAL GOVERNMENT

Electronic procurement governance

Worked with implementation teams to translate procurement rules into electronic workflows, focusing on legal compliance, approval trails, transparency and reliable records. The platform's institutional rollout was an Agency-wide achievement.

Role: Legal and business-process input
