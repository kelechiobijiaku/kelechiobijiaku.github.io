02 / SELECTED CONTRIBUTIONS

Policy, regulatory frameworks and institutional rule books.

Selected examples of team-based work. Descriptions distinguish personal contributions from outcomes delivered by government institutions and partner organisations.

LEGISLATION & REGULATION

Lagos State Public Procurement Law, 2021

Led Agency review and technical coordination work on the Lagos State Public Procurement Law, 2021, and the development and review of its implementing regulations, working with colleagues and relevant institutional stakeholders.

Role: Lead — law and regulations review/development workstreams
POLICY DEVELOPMENT

Gender-responsive procurement policy

Led Lagos technical policy work on the gender-responsive procurement framework, coordinating contributions to policy proposals, eligibility criteria, reporting measures and implementation guidance in collaboration with institutional colleagues and UN Women. The State approved the policy in 2025.

Role: Lead — Lagos technical policy development workstream
TRANSPARENCY & ACCOUNTABILITY

World Bank-supported SFTAS procurement transparency

Coordinated the Lagos procurement workstream for contract-award disclosure and supporting guidance, training and evidence under the SFTAS programme, including OCDS-aligned publication requirements.

Role: State procurement workstream coordination
PROCEDURES & RULE BOOKS

Procurement Manual, guidelines and Vision 2036

Served as a member of the technical team that worked on the Procurement Manual, Emergency Procurement Guidelines, Guidelines on Disposal of Public Assets, Sustainable Procurement Framework and Vision 2036. These were collaborative institutional exercises, not individually authored documents.

Role: Technical team member — collaborative policy and document development
INSTITUTIONAL DESIGN

Process maps and procurement workflows

Led legal-department process mapping and contributed to a statewide procurement process flowchart, clarifying approvals, responsibilities, recordkeeping and handoffs across procurement activities.

Role: Process design and practitioner guidance
DIGITAL GOVERNMENT

Electronic procurement governance

Worked with implementation teams to translate procurement rules into electronic workflows, focusing on legal compliance, approval trails, transparency and reliable records. The platform's institutional rollout was an Agency-wide achievement.

Role: Legal and business-process input

Selected contributions reflect professional involvement; they do not imply sole authorship of government laws, policies, projects or institutional achievements.
