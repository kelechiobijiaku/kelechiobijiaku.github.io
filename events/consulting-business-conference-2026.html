EXPERTISE. ACCESS. OPPORTUNITY.

A clearer route to public sector consulting.

Government needs expertise. How can your consulting business get through the door? Join Kelechi Uzoma Obijiaku for a practical look at identifying public sector opportunities, understanding procurement requirements and competing with confidence. Designed for consultants, consulting firms, entrepreneurs and professionals ready to turn their expertise into credible government contracting opportunities.

The presentation explores

  • Recognise where your expertise meets public sector consulting needs.
  • Understand the procurement requirements that shape access to government contracts.
  • Prepare to compete with a clearer view of opportunities, eligibility and client expectations.
